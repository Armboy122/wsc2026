# Goal

Turn Knowledge into deterministic infrastructure that supplies Gemini Live with complete, trusted, approved local Markdown documents. Gemini Live selects source IDs from a compact deterministic catalog and remains the only component that reasons or answers.

## Out of Scope

No Knowledge router/answer LLM, embeddings, vector database, cloud store, chunking, generated summaries, or alternate conversational agent. Do not change the voice transport or remove unrelated platform modules; Story 1 and Story 3 own those scopes.
