# PEA Service FAQ

Frequently asked questions about PEA electricity service.

## Q: How do I pay my electricity bill?

Pay at any PEA service office, bank branch, ATM with banking service,
convenience store payment point, or online banking. You need the customer
account number printed on the bill. Payment for the previous month is due by
the 20th of the current month.

## Q: How do I report a power outage?

Call the PEA emergency hotline 1165. Provide your area code, a short
location note (street or landmark), and what you observe (no power, partial
power, sparks, burning smell). For a suspected downed power line, keep at
least 3 metres away and do not touch it.

## Q: Why was my bill higher than last month?

The bill reflects actual metered consumption. Higher usage (for example from
air conditioning in hot weather), a longer billing period, or moving into a
higher consumption tier all increase the amount. Compare the "units
consumed" figure on both bills to see the change.

## Q: How do I change the billing period or request a meter reading?

Request a meter reading or a billing change at a PEA service office or
through the service channels with your account number. The standard billing
period remains monthly unless PEA approves a change.

## Q: What are the PEA contact channels?

- Emergency (outages, safety): hotline 1165, available 24 hours.
- Billing and service enquiries: PEA service offices during business hours,
  and the online service channels.

## Q: Can I pay late?

Payment after the due date is possible at any payment channel, but a late
charge of 2% per month applies to the unpaid amount, and unpaid bills are
notified before any service interruption.
