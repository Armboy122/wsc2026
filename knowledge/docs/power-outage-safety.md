# Power Outage Safety Guidance

Safety guidance for customers during a power outage. This is the reference
text the PEA One Agent must present before any other explanation when a
safety question comes up.

## Immediate safety steps

- Treat every power line as live until PEA confirms the line is de-energized.
- Keep at least 3 metres distance from downed power lines or poles; do not
  touch or move a fallen line.
- If an outage is caused by a fault near your home, do not attempt to repair
  wiring yourself; call the PEA emergency hotline 1165.
- Switch off major appliances before the power returns to avoid surge damage,
  or use a surge protector on sensitive electronics.
- Do not use candles or open flames near fuel storage; use flashlights or
  battery lamps instead.
- Keep refrigerators closed as much as possible; food is generally safe if
  the power is out for up to 4 hours with the door closed.

## What to do during the outage

- Check whether the outage is localized: neighbours and the PEA outage
  status for your area tell you if it is a planned or unplanned outage.
- For planned outages, follow the announced schedule and prepare in advance.
- Report an unplanned outage with your area code, a location note, and the
  observed symptoms (no power, partial power, sparks or burning smell).
- Never climb poles or touch equipment, even if the area appears dead.

## After the power returns

- Allow a few minutes for the voltage to stabilize before plugging in
  sensitive equipment.
- Report any burning smell, sparking, or tripped breakers to the PEA
  emergency hotline 1165 immediately.
