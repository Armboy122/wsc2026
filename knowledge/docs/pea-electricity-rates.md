# PEA Electricity Rates and Billing

This document explains how PEA electricity bills are calculated and how
customers can pay.

## How the bill is calculated

- The electricity bill is based on the actual consumption read from the meter
  at the start and end of the billing period, which is normally one month.
- Consumption is charged in tiers: the first 150 units (kWh) per month use
  the lowest tier rate, the next 100 units use a higher rate, and any
  consumption above 300 units per month uses the highest tier rate.
- A fixed monthly charge is added for the meter and service connection,
  separate from the energy charge.
- Electricity excise and value-added tax (VAT) are added on top of the energy
  and fixed charges.
- Late payment of the bill incurs a late charge of 2% per month on the
  unpaid amount.

## Due date and payment

- The bill is issued on the 1st of the month for the previous month's
  consumption, and the payment due date is the 20th of that month.
- Customers can pay at PEA service offices, banks, ATMs with banking
  service, convenience store payment points, or online banking.
- Payment requires the customer account number, which is printed at the top
  of the electricity bill.
- If the bill is not paid by the due date, the customer may be notified
  before any service interruption, and the service interruption happens only
  after the notification period ends.

## Reading the bill

- "Current period reading" minus "previous period reading" gives the units
  consumed this month.
- The bill shows the amount paid for each charge component (energy charge,
  fixed charge, excise, VAT) and the total amount due.
